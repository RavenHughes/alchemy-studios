Founder
The founder · AS / 01-F

J. Daniel Hughes “Raven”

Managing Member & Creative Director, Alchemy Studios, LLC

Alchemy Studios is owner-run. J. Daniel Hughes, known around the shop as Raven, founded DesignWerx, Inc. in 1999 and ran it until 2018, and organized Alchemy Studios, LLC in 2015 to carry that work forward. He sets the direction for every project, and the work on this page passes through his hands before it leaves the studio.

He is entirely self-taught and has spent more than forty years working across disciplines that are usually kept apart: illustration, oil painting, sculpture and calligraphy; props, special effects and fabrication; brand systems and print; software, 3D mechanical design and rapid prototyping. Since 1989 he has done theme-park work of every kind: illustration and concept art, murals, painting and drawing, floor plans and elevations, and branding for Disney's buses, alongside hospital and city-planning work. He went on to lead the studio as a preferred vendor to the parks, has designed and built some 700 platforms and websites, and has taught Photoshop and Illustrator for decades.

Today he directs the studio's own developments, including The Realm, an Unreal Engine world for VR and desktop, and the Apex Passport Card, alongside client brands and builds. The studio's laser lab, woodshop and 3D modeling studio all run under his direction.
