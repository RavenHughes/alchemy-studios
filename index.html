Built for the places people remember

For nearly four decades our team — first as DesignWerx, Inc. and Backstage Technologies, now as Alchemy Studios — has built special effects, themed props, fabrication and branding for some of the most-visited theme parks in the world, including Universal Orlando, Universal Studios Japan, SeaWorld and Dollywood. For The Wizarding World of Harry Potter we sculpted and aged Bludger props and engineered the animated Chocolate Frog box, and for Universal Orlando we built water and fog effects.

Other clients have included ConAgra Foods (Slim Jim), Samsung, Gatorade, Jose Cuervo, Breitling, Daytona International Speedway, Fair Oaks Farms, Leon Air, Loveland Laser Tag, Kemperle of Florida and GT Marine.

The same shop has built climbing walls for national sporting events, branded towers and stages for motorsports, and promotional structures for global brands — and today writes the software behind payment platforms and a full virtual world in Unreal Engine.

Developed by Alchemy Studios

The Realm® is a persistent multiplayer world built in Unreal Engine for VR and desktop: nine islands in a central sea, each with its own purpose, architecture and Land Title collectibles. We design every layer of it, from the world itself to the website, the member systems and the payments.

  • The worldUnreal Engine 5, dedicated servers, first-person VR and flat-screen play.
  • Passport StudioA one-time face scan builds each citizen's passport and likeness, with verified identity keeping the world safe.
  • Land Titles and VMax BrokersCollectible Land Titles for every island, with a broker exchange for members who resell.
  • Virtual learningPolymath University and Kingdom Cove's learning theatre bring classes into the world.
Platform, card art and integration by Alchemy Studios

Apex NEO Card is a patented card platform, protected by design, system and utility patents, that carries one verified member identity across metal and virtual cards. Our team designed the cards, the member platform and the merchant tools around it.

  • Cards and identityMetal and virtual cards tied to a verified member, with tiers and custom designs for partners and nations.
  • Front DeskA point-of-sale and payment gateway for vendors.
  • Apex VCardsDigital business cards with laser-etched aluminum cards to match.
  • Banking partnerApex is a technology platform, not a bank; banking services are provided by our partner bank in London.
